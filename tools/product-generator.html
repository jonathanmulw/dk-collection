2. Add a product

Tip: if this item comes in different colors (e.g. a hoodie in White, Black, Grey), list one photo per color, in the same order as your Colors field above — the site will automatically show the matching photo when a customer taps a color. Upload the actual photo files into your images folder separately; this box just tells the site what each file is called. Leave blank to use a placeholder for now.
