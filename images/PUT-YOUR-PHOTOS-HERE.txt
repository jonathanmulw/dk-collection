Put your product photos in this folder.

Example: images/red-ankara-dress.jpg

Then in js/products.js, set that product's "image" field to:
  "images/red-ankara-dress.jpg"

Tips for good photos on a phone:
- Use natural daylight, near a window, avoid direct harsh sun.
- Use a plain background (a wall, bedsheet, or the floor).
- Keep the item centered and take the photo in portrait orientation.
- Keep file sizes reasonable (under 1MB each) so the site loads fast —
  most phone camera apps have a "smaller file size" or "compress" option.
