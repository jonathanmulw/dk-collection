How to Order & Pay

We keep it simple on purpose — everything happens through WhatsApp and M-Pesa, two things you already know how to use.

01

Select your item

Go to the Products page and choose the item you want. Note down the name, price and size you'd like.

02

Confirm via WhatsApp

Tap "Order via WhatsApp" on the item (or the floating green button on any page). This opens a chat with us, already showing what you'd like to order. We'll confirm stock, size and delivery details with you directly.

03

Pay via M-Pesa Till Number

Once we confirm your order, pay using our M-Pesa Till Number below. Send us your M-Pesa confirmation message on WhatsApp and we'll get your order moving.

How to pay via M-Pesa (step by step)

  1. Go to M-Pesa on your phone.
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa.
  3. Select Buy Goods and Services.
  4. Enter Till Number: 8418310.
  5. Enter the amount agreed on WhatsApp and confirm with your M-Pesa PIN.
  6. Send us a screenshot or forward the M-Pesa confirmation message on WhatsApp.
Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay before you confirm my order?

No. We always confirm item availability, size and delivery details with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

Is my payment safe?

Yes — you pay directly to our official M-Pesa Till Number, so no card or personal payment details are ever shared.

How is my order delivered?

Delivery options and costs are agreed on WhatsApp based on your location, so you always know the total cost upfront.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery if there's an issue with your order and we'll sort it out with you.
